For a returning customer, find them by mobile number, reopen their avatar in Saved
avatars, try clothes and save a reviewed look. Share that saved look after confirming
customer agreement. Changing stores clears the active customer session. Customer
erasure removes their linked avatars, saved looks, adaptations and share links from
the live store database; downloaded copies and external provider/backups have
separate retention boundaries described in the linked guides.
